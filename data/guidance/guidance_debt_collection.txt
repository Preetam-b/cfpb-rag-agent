Internal Complaint-Handling Guidance
Category: Debt Collection

When a customer complains about debt collection contact:
1. Determine if the customer has received a validation notice for the debt. If not, provide one before continuing collection communication.
2. If the customer disputes owing the debt, pause active collection until the debt is validated.
3. Check contact frequency: repeated or continuous calls intended to annoy, abuse, or harass a customer are prohibited, regardless of the channel (phone, text, email).
4. If the customer requests no further contact, log this immediately and route to the compliance team before any further communication attempts.
5. If the customer indicates the debt may belong to someone deceased, route to the estate-handling procedure rather than continuing standard collection.
6. Escalate any complaint alleging harassment to a human reviewer, regardless of confidence in resolving it automatically.
