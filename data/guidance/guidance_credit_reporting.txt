Internal Complaint-Handling Guidance
Category: Credit Reporting

When a customer disputes information on their credit report:
1. Identify whether the dispute is about an account they don't recognize, incorrect balance/terms, or suspected identity theft.
2. Request supporting documentation only if the customer hasn't already provided enough to identify the account and the basis of the dispute (e.g., a police report for identity theft).
3. Do not require a specific proprietary form before investigating; a written or verbal dispute with enough detail must be accepted.
4. Conduct a reasonable investigation of the disputed information — do not close a dispute as "verified" without checking the underlying record.
5. Do not mark a dispute as frivolous unless it clearly lacks any specific claim or supporting basis.
6. Communicate the outcome to the customer in plain language, including what was changed on their report, if anything.
