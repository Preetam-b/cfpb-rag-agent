Internal Complaint-Handling Guidance
Category: Credit Card

When a customer disputes a credit card charge:
1. Confirm the charge date, amount, and merchant name with the customer.
2. Check whether the charge matches a known billing error type: unauthorized use, incorrect amount, or a charge for goods/services never received.
3. If unauthorized use is confirmed, cap the customer's liability at $50 and begin the billing-error investigation within 30 days of the notice.
4. If the dispute involves a merchant issue (wrong item, non-delivery), advise the customer they may also contact the merchant directly, but the bank must still investigate under billing-error rules if requested in writing.
5. Provide a written acknowledgment within 30 days and resolve within two billing cycles, or 90 days, whichever is sooner.
6. Do not close the dispute as resolved until the customer confirms the outcome or the regulatory deadline is reached.
