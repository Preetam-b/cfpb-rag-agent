Internal Complaint-Handling Guidance
Category: Retail Banking

When a customer disputes an electronic fund transfer or account issue:
1. Determine if the dispute involves an unauthorized transfer, an incorrect transfer amount, or a transfer missing from a statement.
2. If the customer reports a lost or stolen access device, apply liability limits based on how quickly it was reported: $50 if reported within two business days, higher limits if reported later.
3. Error-resolution procedures still apply even if the customer's account has since been closed.
4. Do not treat a request for transaction documentation as anything other than an error notice unless the customer clearly states it's for personal record-keeping only.
5. Provide provisional credit to the customer's account if the investigation will take longer than the standard resolution window, per applicable rules.
6. Confirm final resolution with the customer directly, not just through an automated notice.
