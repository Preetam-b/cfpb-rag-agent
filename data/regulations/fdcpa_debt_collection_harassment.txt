Regulation F - 12 CFR 1006.14 - Harassing, Oppressive, or Abusive Conduct (Debt Collection)
Category: Debt Collection

(a) In general. A debt collector must not engage in any conduct the natural consequence of which is to harass, oppress, or abuse any person in connection with the collection of a debt.

(b) Repeated or continuous telephone calls or telephone conversations.
(1) In general. A debt collector must not place telephone calls or engage any person in telephone conversation repeatedly or continuously with intent to annoy, abuse, or harass any person at the called number.

This prohibition applies regardless of the communication medium used, including in-person interactions, phone calls, mail, email, text messages, or social media, even if the specific conduct is not separately listed as prohibited.
