Regulation E - 12 CFR 1005.11 - Procedures for Resolving Errors (Retail Banking / EFT)
Category: Retail Banking

(a) Definition of error. The term "error" means:
(i) An unauthorized electronic fund transfer;
(ii) An incorrect electronic fund transfer to or from the consumer's account;
(iii) The omission of an electronic fund transfer from a periodic statement;
(iv) A computational or bookkeeping error made by the financial institution relating to an electronic fund transfer;
(v) The consumer's receipt of an incorrect amount of money from an electronic terminal;
(vii) The consumer's request for documentation or additional information to determine whether an error exists.

A financial institution must comply with error resolution procedures even if the account has been closed, and even when a consumer reports loss or theft of an access device along with possible unauthorized use.
